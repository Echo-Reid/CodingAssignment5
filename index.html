Roast Beef French Dips

Recipe by: Echo Reid

Ingredients:

  • 8 ounces of sliced, precooked roast beef (per sandwich).
  • 8 inch french rolls (1 per sandwich, can be substituted for any comparably sized bread).
  • 1 slice of swiss cheese (per sandwich).
  • 1/4 ounce of shredded pepperjack cheese (per sandwich).
  • 1/8 ounce of mayonaise (per sandwich).
  • 2 quarts of au jus (per 8 sandwiches, but don't make less than 2 quarts)

Equipment:

  • A scale.
  • An oven.
  • A stove and pasta cooker set with inserts
    (acceptable substitutions: any pot and strainer that can hold and withstand the au jus at 135 degrees).
  • A small pan
    (needs to hold the pasta cooking insert/strainer).
  • A cooking thermometer.
  • A cutting board.
  • A sharp knife.
  • An 8 ounce ladle.
  • A set of tongs.
  • A timer.
  • For serving, plates and 10 ounce soup cups.

Directions:

  1. Arrange a rack in the center of the oven. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Start to bring the au jus to 135 degrees farenheit on the stove (use the thermometer to check).
  3. Portion the sliced roast beef into 8 ounce portions using the scale.
  4. Slice the french rolls horizontally, if they aren't already.
  5. Spread 1/8 ounce of mayonaise on the bottom halves of the rolls.
  6. Cut the slice of swiss cheese in half diagonally, and spread it, along with 1/8 ounce of the shredded pepperjack, evenly over the top halfs of the rolls.
  7. If necessary, wait for the oven to finish preheating and the au jus to reach 135 degrees.
  8. Place 1-3 full rolls (top and bottom) in the oven. Start a timer for 7 minutes.
  9. Quickly place 1-3 portions of roast beef into the pasta insert. Ideally 1 portion for every roll you placed in the oven. You can add more, but be aware that you will have to seperate the portions. Put the insert into the au jus, so that all of the beef is submerged.
  10. Once the timer is up, move the rolls from the oven to the cutting board. Remove the pasta cooking insert from the au jus and let it sit in the small pan to drain. Using tongs, move the roast beef portions into the baked rolls.
  11. Repeat steps 8-10 until all of the sandwiches are cooked.
  12. Slice the sandwiches across the width or diagonally. Use the ladle to fill the small cups with au jus. Plate and serve.
